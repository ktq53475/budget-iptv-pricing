Price gets you onto the shortlist. It should not be the only thing you check.

First, test the channels you actually watch. Then check how the service behaves on your own device and connection. Look at the EPG, try live channels at different times, and make sure the number of simultaneous connections matches how your household watches TV.

TiviLive's terms also state that it operates as a **reseller rather than a broadcaster**, that content availability can change, and that the service requires a compatible device and stable internet connection.
